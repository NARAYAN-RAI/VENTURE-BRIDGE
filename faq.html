Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to view how our formulas and funding process work.

We use realistic industry multiples on Yearly Revenue (ARR): a $250K base cap for Idea stage, 5x ARR for Pre-Seed, and 10x–15x ARR for Working startups (plus a 2x bonus on net yearly profit).

Idea stage startups receive a fixed $50,000 Launchpad grant. Pre-Seed startups receive 15% of their valuation (capped at $250K), and Working startups receive 12% of their valuation plus 50% of their yearly net profit (capped at $1M).

Our Smart Equity Calculator subtracts both existing investor shares and new round dilution so you can verify that founders still retain over 50% majority control.

Every pitch submitted on our Submit Pitch page receives a guaranteed decision within 14 calendar days.
